## Storyboard & Timing Breakdown

| Time Code | Section Title | Judging Lens | Screen Action | Narration Script |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:30** | Hook & Core Problem | Creativity & Purpose | Show side-by-side: NASA color map vs. conventional pixel sonifier. | *"Every day, NASA captures our changing planet in stunning visualizations. But when traditional sonifiers turn these images into sound, they map raw pixel colors to pitch. Because rainbow palettes are arbitrary, that sound tells you nothing about the true science. Enter Earth Jukebox: we invert the colorbar to recover real data, then sonify the physical measurements."* |
| **0:30 – 1:15** | Data Recovery & Playback | Technical Rigour | Open Earth Jukebox web app. Select global SST anomaly map. Press Play. Watch playhead sweep W to E. | *"Let's select this global Sea Surface Temperature anomaly map from the Jukebox gallery. Earth Jukebox extracts the color legend, builds a perceptual CIE L\*a\*b\* lookup tree, and recovers the physical field with sub-percent error. Press Play: As the playhead sweeps West to East across longitude, each latitude band sings through a distinct stereo voice. Pitch directly reflects the temperature anomaly in degrees Celsius."* |
| **0:15 – 1:45** | Audio Legend & Cultural Skins | Musicality & Creativity | Click 'Audio Legend'. Then switch skin from Neutral to Hindustani, then Sri Lankan. | *"How do you know what the pitches mean? Click 'Audio Legend'. You hear an ascending 32-note scale from minimum to maximum, training your ears to the scale. Now, switch skins to Hindustani Raga or traditional Sri Lankan drums. Notice: the aesthetic expression transforms, but the underlying data remains invariant. Temperature highs and lows sound at the exact same moments."* |
| **1:45 – 2:30** | Accessibility in Action | Universal Design & Inclusivity | Close eyes / switch to keyboard mode. Use arrow keys to navigate grid. Turn on Haptics. Show Fidelity Panel. | *"Accessibility is central to our design, not an afterthought. In Explore Mode, blind and low-vision users navigate the data grid cell by cell using only the keyboard. Instant speech announcements report coordinates and exact physical values, while tactile haptic vibrations pulse in direct proportion to data intensity. Open the Fidelity Panel to inspect our mathematical proof: 0.32% recovery error and 0.99 pitch correlation."* |
| **2:30 – 3:00** | Time Mode & Multi-Format Export | Innovation & Polish | Switch to Time Mode on multi-frame hurricane sequence. Click Download WAV / MP3 / MIDI. | *"In Time Mode, multi-frame animations become dynamic musical phrases. When climate patterns accelerate, inter-frame variance introduces microtonal harmonic tension into the drone bed. Finally, researchers and educators can export the soundtrack as studio WAV, MP3, MIDI, or pure Score JSON. Earth Jukebox: turning NASA's visual data into an accessible, musical language for everyone."* |
